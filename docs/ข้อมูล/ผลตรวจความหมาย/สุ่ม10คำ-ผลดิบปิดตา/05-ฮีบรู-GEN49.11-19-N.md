הוּא
Piece 2 (וֹ) is the third-person masculine singular pronominal suffix functioning as a possessive modifier meaning "his" [1]. The whole word לְבֻשׁ֔וֹ means "his garment," referring to the clothing washed in wine within Judah's poetic blessing of agricultural abundance [2]. This pronominal suffix is cataloged under the headword הוּא because standard Biblical Hebrew reference lexicons and grammatical databases group third-person personal suffixes under their corresponding independent personal pronoun [1].
