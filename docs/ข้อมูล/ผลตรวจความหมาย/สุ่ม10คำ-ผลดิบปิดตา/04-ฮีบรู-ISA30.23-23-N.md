אַתָּה
Piece 2 (ךָ) is the second-person masculine singular pronominal suffix functioning as a possessive modifier meaning "your" [1, 2]. The whole word מִקְנֶ֛יךָ means "your cattle" or "your livestock," referring to the herds belonging to the addressed hearer in this context of agricultural abundance [3]. This suffix is lexicographically mapped to the headword אַתָּה because standard Biblical Hebrew reference systems and databases catalog second-person pronominal suffixes under their corresponding independent personal pronouns [1, 4].
