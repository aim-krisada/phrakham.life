הוּא
Piece 2 (ו) is the third-person masculine singular pronominal suffix meaning "its" or "his," which refers back to the masculine singular noun for "wickedness" in the clause. The whole word בְּעָלָֽיו means "its possessors" or "its owners" (contextually "those who practice it"), utilizing a plural of ownership or extension combined with this singular suffix. The suffix is lexicographically assigned to the headword הוּא because Hebrew databases and lexicons traditionally catalog all third-person pronominal suffixes under their corresponding independent personal pronouns.
