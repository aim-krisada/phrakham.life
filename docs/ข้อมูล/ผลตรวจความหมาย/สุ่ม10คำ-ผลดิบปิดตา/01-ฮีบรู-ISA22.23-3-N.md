הוּא
Piece 3 (ו) is the third-person masculine singular pronominal suffix functioning as the direct object "him," which refers back to Eliakim. The whole word וּתְקַעְתִּ֥יו translates to "and I will fasten him," combining a conjunction, a Qal perfect first-person singular verb, and the object suffix. The suffix is lexicographically mapped to the headword הוּא because standard Biblical Hebrew reference materials catalog third-person pronominal suffixes under their corresponding independent personal pronouns.
