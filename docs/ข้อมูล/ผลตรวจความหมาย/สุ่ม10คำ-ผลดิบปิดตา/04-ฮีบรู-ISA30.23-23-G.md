Piece 2 (ךָ) is the 2nd person masculine singular pronominal suffix meaning "your" or "of you". The full word מִקְנֶ֛יךָ means "your livestock" or "your cattle" in Isaiah 30:23. Lexicons do not list pronominal suffixes as independent headwords; instead, personal pronouns are entry headings under the core independent form אַתָּה (you). Thus, a reader opening a entry for this suffix should be directed to the headword אַתָּה.
